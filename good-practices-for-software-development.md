### Question 2

#### Which area do you think you need to improve on? What are your ideas to make progress in that area?

I need to improve on asking for help when I am stuck. I sometimes spend too much time on a problem because I feel I should work it out myself. I want to improve by first spending some time understanding and trying to solve the issue, noting down what I tried and what errors I am getting, and then asking my teammates with enough context.
