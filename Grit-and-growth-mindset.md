### Question 2
Paraphrase (summarise) the video about growth mindset in a few (1 or 2) lines in your own words.

  The video is essentially about the difference between a fixed mindset and a growth mindset. People with a fixed mindset believe their abilities are naturally fixed, while people with a growth mindset believe they can develop their skills through learning, effort, and practice.

### Question 3
What is the Internal Locus of Control? What is the key point in the video?

  Internal locus of control means believing that your actions and choices have a strong influence on the outcomes in your life, rather than blaming external factors. The key point of the video is that having an internal locus of control helps you stay motivated without depending on external motivation.
