### What kinds of behaviour cause sexual harassment?
- Inappropriate comments, jokes, gestures, staring, unwanted touching
- Comments about someone's appearance or personal life if the person is not comfortable
- Sending inappropriate messages, images or emails


### What would you do in case you face or witness any incident or repeated incidents of such behaviour?.
I would first talk to the person that I am not comfortable with it. I would remember the incident and details and note them down as well, and keep any relevant messages or other evidence. I would then report the incident to the appropriate person or committee.

If I witness such behaviour happening to someone else, I would support the person and encourage them to report it. I would avoid spreading the incident among colleagues and would respect the privacy of everyone involved.
