# Learning process  
Feynman technique is a learning method in which we learn as if we are teaching a child and find gaps in our understanding.

In the video, an interesting story I found was about how Dali, a painter, switches from diffuse mode to active mode and starts working on his painting. He relaxes and lets the ideas run in his mind, and he relaxes till he is about to fall asleep, and as soon as the keys are in his hands and fall down, he turns into active mode and works on the painting.

Active modes of thinking are when our attention is on, and diffuse mode is when we relax and let the ideas flow.

### 4 Steps to approach to learn anything
- Deconstruct skill
- Learn enough to self-correct
- Remove practice barriers
- Practice at least 20 hours

### Actions I take to improve my learning process
- Remove distractions
- Keep track of my tasks
- Explain what I learned as if explaining to a child
- Keep Practicing
