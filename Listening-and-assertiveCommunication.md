### Steps to do active listening
- Avoid distractions from your own thoughts
- Focus on the speaker and the topic
- Don't interrupt the other person
- Let them finish and respond
- Use door openers
- Show them you're listening with body language
- Paraphrase what others have said

### Reflective listening means listening and explaining back, so the other person knows that you understood
Its key points are
- Focus on their meaning and feelings
- Repeat what you understood
- Check your understanding by asking
- Show empathy
- Avoid judging

### Obstacles in my listening are
- Tends to interrupt with contradicting points
- Gets distracted by my own thoughts
- Not showing body language

I am improving my listening by focusing on the speaker and what they say, correcting my body language and repeating what I have understood.

We switch to passive communication when we experience fear, anger, anxiety, etc to hide our feelings.

We switch to assertive communication when we are confident and let others know our feelings and boundaries.

### How to make communication assertive
- Learn to name feelings
- Learn to recognise and name what you need
- Be aware of your body
- Don't wait.
