### Question 1

#### What is Deep Work?
Deep work is when we are focused without any distractions, and coding is a classic example of this, we are deeply involved in it to solve a problem or build an application.

### Question 2

#### According to the author, how to do deep work properly, in a few points?
Deep work can be done with 3 strategies.
- Schedule distractions: Fix a time for distractions so you can maintain boundaries and limit distractions
- Deep work ritual: Transform it into a habit by doing it daily for at least one hour
- Evening shutdown: Sleep is most important for doing deep work, as our brain needs to be active for long hours. Make a list of unfinished tasks before sleep, so that we can continue deep work the next day

### Question 3

#### How can you implement the principles in your day-to-day life?
- Schedule distractions after office hours or during lunch break
- Do a deep work ritual early in the morning when the mind feels fresh and active.
- Before sleep, note down the list of unfinished tasks and make sure to get a good amount of sleep
